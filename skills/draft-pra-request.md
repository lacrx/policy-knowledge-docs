# Draft a CPRA Public Records Request

Write a CPRA-compliant public records request targeting specific government records.
Follow steps in order.

---

## Prerequisites

- Know the **agency** (city, county, special district)
- Know the **subject** (project name, policy decision, department action)
- Know the **approximate date range** (90 days default; expand if needed)
- Know the **record types** you want (emails, staff reports, contracts, permits, etc.)

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify the records custodian

Find the agency's designated CPRA contact. Usually the city clerk's office.

```
City Clerk
[City Name]
[Address]
```

If unknown, address to "Records Custodian" — the agency must route internally.

### Step 2: Define the record scope

For each category of records, specify:
- **Record type**: emails, memoranda, staff reports, contracts, meeting recordings, permits, plan sets, correspondence, text messages
- **Parties**: between/among whom (named individuals, departments, external parties)
- **Subject**: the specific project, policy, or decision
- **Date range**: start and end dates (use absolute dates, not "last 3 months")

**Common targeting patterns:**

| Goal | Request |
|------|---------|
| Council influence on a project | All correspondence between council members and [developer/applicant] regarding [project] |
| Staff analysis behind a decision | All staff reports, memoranda, and analyses regarding [item] prepared for [meeting date] |
| Permit history | All permit applications, plan review comments, and inspection records for [address/APN] |
| Budget decisions | All budget worksheets, department requests, and council direction regarding [program] for FY [year] |
| Developer negotiations | All correspondence, agreements, and meeting notes between [city department] and [entity] regarding [project] |

### Step 3: Draft the request

Use this template. Fill in bracketed fields.

```
[Date]

[Agency Name]
Attn: Records Custodian / City Clerk
[Address]

RE: Request for Public Records Pursuant to the California Public Records Act

Dear Records Custodian:

Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7921.000 et seq.),
I request copies of the following public records:

1. All [record type] between, among, or involving [parties] regarding [subject]
   for the period [start date] through [end date].

2. All [record type] regarding [subject] for the period [start date] through
   [end date].

[Add numbered items as needed]

This request includes records in any format, including but not limited to
paper documents, electronic files, emails, text messages, and voicemails.

Please provide all responsive records in electronic format pursuant to
Gov. Code § 7922.570(b).

Per Gov. Code § 7922.535(a), I expect a determination regarding disclosable
records within 10 calendar days of your receipt of this request. If any
records are withheld, please identify the specific exemption claimed and
the records to which it applies, and provide all reasonably segregable
non-exempt portions per Gov. Code § 7922.525.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this request.

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Email]
[Phone]
```

### Step 4: Delivery

- **Email** to the city clerk AND the department most likely to hold records
- **CC** the city attorney's office (signals you know the law)
- **Subject line**: "CPRA Request — [Subject] — [Date]"
- Save the sent email with timestamp as your proof of submission

### Step 5: Track the response

| Day | Expected Action |
|-----|----------------|
| 0 | Request submitted |
| 10 | Determination due (§ 7922.535(a)) |
| 11 | If no response: send follow-up citing missed deadline |
| 24 | Extended deadline expires (if extension was properly invoked) |
| 25 | If still no response: send demand letter citing violation |
| 30+ | Consider Superior Court petition (§ 7923.000) |

### Step 6: Handle pushback

- **Fee demand**: Respond that CPRA limits to direct duplication costs (§ 7922.530). Request electronic format (zero marginal cost).
- **"Too vague"**: Narrow the date range or add specificity, but do NOT withdraw the request.
- **"Extension needed"**: Acceptable only with written notice stating the specific unusual circumstance (§ 7922.535(b)).
- **Blanket denial**: Demand the specific exemption, the specific records withheld, and remind of partial disclosure obligation.

---

## Constraints

| Constraint | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Never ask for conclusions | Request records, not agency analysis. "Records showing X violated Y" invites the agency to decide nothing is responsive. |
| Always request electronic format | Prevents per-page fee gambit and produces searchable records |
| Use absolute dates | "Last 90 days" is ambiguous; "March 1, 2026 through June 28, 2026" is not |
| Name parties broadly | "Between or among" catches CC'd parties; "from [person]" misses inbound correspondence |

## Outputs

- A ready-to-send CPRA request email with proper statutory citations
- A tracking timeline for follow-up
- Response templates for common agency pushback tactics
